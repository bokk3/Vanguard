## ⚡ Narrative Core Pillars

```text
       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │                   NARRATIVE THESIS                     │
       │  "Humanity climbed into the stars with precision CAD.   │
       │   Now they defend the sky with raw kinetic blood."     │
       └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Grounded Void:** Space is not a vacuum of silent magic; it is an unforgiving operating environment governed by heat dissipation, fuel capacitors, aerodynamic lift envelopes, and orbital mechanics.
2. **The Human Factor in the Swarm Age:** In an era where corporate cartels deploy billions of autonomous AI drones, manned interceptor pilots represent the only unpredictable, non-hackable variable in the sky.
3. **Industrial Aerospace Aesthetic:** Everything looks manufactured, stamped, bolted, and tuned by aerospace engineers. No superfluous fantasy wings—every fin, chine, intake, and nozzle serves a thermodynamic or aerodynamic purpose.
