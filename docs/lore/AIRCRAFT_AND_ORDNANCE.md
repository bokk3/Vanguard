# 🛸 Aircraft & Ordnance Dossiers

All operational aerospace craft in Project Vanguard are precision-engineered to bridge aerodynamic atmospheric flight with vacuum combat readiness.

## 1. F-77 Sculpted V-Hull Interceptor ("Vanguard Interceptor")

```text
Length: 10.30 m  |  Wingspan: 9.78 m  |  Height: 2.70 m  |  Dry Mass: 14,200 kg
Propulsion: Twin Magnetoplasmadynamic (MPD) Afterburning Turborockets
Primary Role: Multi-Role High-Speed Atmospheric Interceptor / Low-Orbit Breach Craft
```

### Engineering & Aerodynamics
* **7-Station Transverse 3D Lofting:** The airframe features a compound V-keel with zero flat underside surfaces, allowing continuous high-speed compression lift at supersonic velocities while reducing radar cross-section.
* **Ventral Weapons Tunnel:** A recessed ventral channel cradles ordnance, keeping drag minimal during dense cloud-layer maneuvers.
* **Avionics & Sensor Suite:**
  * **Dual Forward Projectors:** High-intensity spotlight array for deep-canyon zero-light navigation.
  * **Ventral Diamond Pod (FLIR / EOTS):** Electro-Optical Targeting System with $360^\circ$ continuous gimbal coverage for multi-target missile locking.
  * **Faceted Canopy:** Anti-glare polarized composite crystal offering pilots an unobstructed $270^\circ$ visual envelope.

### Armament & Hardpoints
* **Station 1 & 4 (Outer Pylons):** Swept pylons wired for long-range kinetic or strike ordnance.
* **Station 2 & 3 (Inner Pylons):** Heavy-load pylon mounts configured for the **Vanguard Precision Strike Missile**.
* **Nose Mount:** Internal 20mm High-Velocity Rotary Cannon ($1,200\text{ rounds/min}$) synchronized with HUD lead-computing reticles.
