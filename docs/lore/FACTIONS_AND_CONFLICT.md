# ⚔️ Factions & The Global Theater

The conflict in **Project Vanguard** unfolds across the upper atmosphere, planetary ascension corridors, and low orbital platforms. The skies are partitioned between three primary entities:

---

## 1. The Sol Orbital Directorate (SOD)
* **Designation:** Sovereign Coalition of Planetary Governments & Orbital Authorities
* **Motto:** *"Astra Nostra Vincunt"* (Our Stars Prevail)
* **Insignia / Colors:** Cobalt Blue (`#0D47A1`), Titanium White (`#ECEFF1`), High-Visibility Hazard Amber (`#FFB300`)
* **Philosophy:** Centralized constitutional order, strict environmental control of orbital staging areas, and human-in-the-loop military doctrine.
* **Military Arm:** The **Orbital Defense Command (ODC)**. Controls the massive orbital mass drivers, orbital elevators, and equatorial catapult corridors.

```text
       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │             SOL ORBITAL DIRECTORATE (SOD)              │
       │  "Without orbital supremacy, civilization starves."     │
       └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. The 404th Tactical Strike Wing ("VANGUARD")
* **Status:** Elite Special Operations Aerospace Unit (Player's Faction)
* **Home Base:** Mobile Super-Carrier *SOC Dauntless* & High-Altitude Catapult Outpost *Apex-Zero*
* **Callsign Prefix:** `Vanguard [Flight #]` / `Apex Command` (AWACS)
* **Role:** The rapid-reaction tip of the spear. When automated drone grids fail or are compromised by electronic warfare, the Vanguard flight is scrambled into the breach.
* **Doctrinal Strength:** High-energy combat maneuvering, hybrid atmospheric-to-orbital interception, precision strike missile delivery, and close-in rotary cannon dogfighting.

---

## 3. The Helion Extraction Combine (The Combine)
* **Designation:** Rogue Trans-Orbital Resource Conglomerate & Black-Market Extraction Syndicate
* **Motto:** *"Wealth Unbound by Atmosphere"*
* **Insignia / Colors:** Matte Carbon Black (`#212121`), Tactical Crimson Red (`#D50000`), Burnished Copper (`#B87333`)
* **Philosophy:** Unrestricted exploitation of planetary resources, non-compliance with Directorate orbital tariffs, and complete reliance on automated warfare.
* **Tactics:**
  * **Autonomous Swarm Grids:** High-volume, low-cost target drones (`target_drone.gd`) programmed to overwhelm conventional air defense with saturation attacks.
  * **Hit-and-Run Interdictions:** Striking planetary mining shafts, research corridors, and orbital lift vehicles before vanishing into high orbital trajectories.
  * **Electronic Warfare:** Heavy use of sensor ghosting, reticle jamming, and communications blackout fields.

---

## 4. The Frontier Freeborn (Neutral / Mercenary Enclave)
* **Role:** Asteroid belt miners, freelance engineers, and orbital smugglers operating in the gray zone.
* **Relevance:** Source of experimental weapon retrofits, customized engine capacitors, and contested neutral trading ports.
