# Weapons and Hardpoints

## 1. Overview

Ordnance is modeled parametrically in Fusion 360, then given collision and sockets in Blender 4.2, and exported for Godot 4 and Unreal Engine 5.

```mermaid
graph TD
    FusionCAD[Fusion 360: Vanguard_Strike_Missile.step] --> BlenderRig[Blender 4.2: PBR Shaders & Sockets]
    BlenderRig --> StandaloneFBX[Standalone Asset: Vanguard_Strike_Missile.fbx]
    BlenderRig --> FullCombatShip[Mounted Combat Fighter: Spaceship_Sculpted_V_Hull.fbx / .glb]
    
    FullCombatShip --> GodotEngine[Godot 4: 4x Hardpoint Nodes]
    FullCombatShip --> UnrealEngine[Unreal 5: 4x StaticMesh Sockets]
```

* **Dimensions:** Length $1.18\text{ m}$, Height $0.148\text{ m}$, Width $0.052\text{ m}$.
* **Leading Edge:** swept $45^\circ$.
* Sits flush against the underside of the wing.

The fighter has 4 under-wing hardpoints. These are the socket positions in the Blender/UE5 asset (Blender axes):

## 5. Launching Missiles

### Godot
`spaceship_controller.gd` builds the hardpoints at runtime in
`_setup_weapon_hardpoints()` rather than reading the sockets from the mesh. The
positions are in Godot space:

| Station | Position |
| :--- | :--- |
| Left outer | `(-3.2, -0.22, 1.2)` |
| Left inner | `(-2.2, -0.26, 0.4)` |
| Right inner | `(2.2, -0.26, 0.4)` |
| Right outer | `(3.2, -0.22, 1.2)` |

Missiles are instances of `missile.tscn` (`missile.gd`). `LAUNCH_SEQUENCE`
fires left outer, right outer, left inner, then right inner to keep the load
balanced. The rack model on the firing station is hidden, and
`update_missile_racks()` shows it again as missiles reload.

### Unreal Engine 5
* `Get Socket Transform` on `SM_Spaceship_Sculpted_V_Hull` with socket
  `SOCKET_Weapon_Hardpoint_01` (to `_04`).
* `SpawnActorFromClass` (`BP_Vanguard_Strike_Missile`) at that transform.
* Attach the Niagara exhaust to `SOCKET_Missile_Exhaust`.
